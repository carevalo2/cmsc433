Types first, followed by code from signatures, monadic execution, State,
verification conditions, laziness, and a mixed set.
\vfill
\textbf{How to answer}\par
Open \code{study-guide.tex} and the relevant folder's \code{lesson.tex} in your
editor. Replace the text inside \code{\answer{...}} for written answers.
For coding questions, replace the \code{undefined} lines inside
\code{codeanswer} blocks with your Haskell implementation. All answers can be
written in LaTeX; no separate Haskell file is required. Rebuild the PDF with
\code{.\build.ps1}. The companion \code{Practice.hs} files are optional for GHCi
checks. The PDF is a compiled reading copy, not a fillable PDF form.
\par\medskip
\textbf{Practice basis}\par
Spring 2025 sample Q1--Q5 and local lectures. No official Fall 2026 scope,
timing, resource rules, or weighting is assumed. The mixed set matches the
sample's question styles, not a verified current exam format. No solutions are
included. Reference signatures are at the end.
